About

Shea Johnson LLC is a Boston-based management consulting firm specializing in AI training and
workforce development. We help government agencies successfully adopt artificial intelligence
through practical workshops, custom e-learning programs, and strategic consulting.
As a certified Minority and Women-Owned Business Enterprise (M/WBE), we bring both technical
expertise and supplier diversity value to every engagement. Our founder has trained over 200
professionals through partnerships with SCORE and delivers hands-on programs that turn AI skeptics
into confident users.
